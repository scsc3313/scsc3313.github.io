What Dear does

Daily Couple Question

Share what’s been on your mind, and Dear creates a question that fits the two of you perfectly. The things hard to bring up become conversations that bring you closer.

Heart Post Office

Write what you truly feel, even when it’s hard to say. Dear gently rewrites harsh or confusing words into warm messages your partner will receive with love.

Couple Calendar

Never forget anniversaries, birthdays, or the day you met. Shared events keep the two of you in sync.

Daily Fortune

A light, fun daily reading for you, your partner, and your couple energy, inspired by traditional Korean astrology.

Private and Secure

Biometric and device-PIN lock protect your conversations. Your story stays between the two of you.

Premium

An ad-free experience, unlimited daily questions and translations, and benefits automatically shared with your partner.
