Child Safety Standards

App: Dear - AI Couple Counselor
Developer: seungHo Hyun
Effective date: April 22, 2026
Last updated: April 22, 2026

Dear - AI Couple Counselor ("the App", "we", "us") has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This document describes our standards, prevention measures, and reporting procedures in compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy for Social and Dating apps.

1. Zero-Tolerance Commitment

We strictly prohibit the creation, upload, sharing, solicitation, or distribution of any content that sexually exploits or abuses children within the App. This includes, without limitation:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form (text, image, audio, video, AI-generated, or illustrated)
  • Grooming, sextortion, or any attempt to sexualize minors
  • Trafficking, solicitation, or recruitment of minors for sexual purposes
  • Sharing personal contact information of minors for sexual purposes
  • Any other content or conduct that endangers the safety or well-being of a child

2. Age Restriction

The App is intended exclusively for users aged 16 and older who are in, or interested in, a romantic relationship. The App is not directed at, designed for, or marketed to children under 13. Users under 16 are not permitted to create an account. If we discover that an account belongs to a user below the minimum age, we will terminate the account and delete associated data.

3. Prevention Measures

We implement the following measures to prevent CSAE/CSAM:

  • Account-gated access: All features require authenticated sign-in via Google Sign-In.
  • Couple-pairing model: Private content (Q&A, Heart Mail, chat, calendar) is only visible to the two paired partners and cannot be broadcast or shared publicly within the App.
  • Content moderation: User-generated text content is subject to automated and manual review when reported. AI-generated responses are filtered to avoid sexual content involving minors.
  • No public social graph: The App does not allow discovery of, messaging with, or friend requests between strangers. Users can only interact with their single invited partner.
  • Account termination: Any user found violating these standards will be permanently banned and, where legally required, reported to law enforcement.

4. In-App Reporting

Users can report child-safety concerns, CSAE, CSAM, or any inappropriate content directly from within the App:

  • Open the App → Settings → Report / Contact
  • Select the category "Child safety concern"
  • Describe the issue and (optionally) attach evidence

5. Our Response Process

When a report is received, we will:

  1. Acknowledge receipt and begin review within 72 hours.
  2. Preserve the reported content and associated account data for investigation.
  3. Remove violating content immediately upon confirmation.
  4. Suspend or permanently terminate the offending account.
  5. Report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org, and to local authorities as required by applicable law.
  6. Cooperate fully with law enforcement requests for information.

6. Legal Compliance

We comply with all applicable child-safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • 18 U.S.C. § 2258A (U.S. reporting requirements to NCMEC)
  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Act on the Protection of Children and Youth Against Sex Offenses (Republic of Korea)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — Article 8 (child consent)
  • Google Play Developer Program Policies — Child Safety Standards

We report suspected CSAM to NCMEC (for users in the United States and globally where permitted) and to the Korea Communications Standards Commission (방송통신심의위원회) or the Korean National Police Agency (사이버범죄 신고시스템 ECRM, ecrm.police.go.kr) for incidents involving users in the Republic of Korea.
